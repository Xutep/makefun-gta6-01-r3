Marisol City — a complete open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6, built for
make.fun's "Make GTA 6" bounty: a sun-soaked coastal city to explore on foot and by
car, with traffic and pedestrians, cars to steal, a wanted level and police, five
story missions, combat, a HUD with minimap, a start screen and saves. Playable in a
desktop browser from static files, no network needed.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```

## Controls

- **WASD / arrows** — walk and drive · **Shift** — run
- **E** — enter / exit a car · **F** — punch · **Mouse + click** — aim and shoot
- **Space** — handbrake · **C** — camera zoom · **M** — mute · **Esc / P** — pause

## The city

Five districts on one map: downtown high-rises, residential blocks, a park, a
beachfront and a harbour. Traffic follows the road network, pedestrians walk the
sidewalks and flee trouble, and the police answer every crime with a wanted
level from 0 to 5 stars — roadblocks from 3 stars up. Five story missions take
Jax from his first fare to the big score.
